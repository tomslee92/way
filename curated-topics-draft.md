# curated-topics-draft.md — First five curated sessions

> Draft anchor-and-orbit sketches for the next five curated topics, ordered
> by Jesus's emphasis and filtered for threadability (the Emmaus shape).
> Companion to curriculum-seed.md — same structure as the built "I AM" topic.
>
> **These are candidates, not finished curricula.** Per Way's governing
> principle ("assert only what you've authored"), every verse below must be
> verified by Toms against ESV (esv.org) and 개역개정 before use, and given a
> `passageId` for API.Bible. The references and station mapping are the
> scholarly-standard biblical-theology thread; the *vouching* is yours.

## Conventions (carried from "I AM")

- **Anchor** = the one verse the user memorizes. Always Jesus speaking.
- **Orbit** = 5 supporting verses, read/illuminated by Rhema, never memorized,
  in canonical order mapped to: origin → unfolding → moment → aftermath →
  consummation.
- **The thread** = one line naming how the topic lands in Christ.
- Shared orbit verses across topics are fine — that's the threading working
  (see library-spec §0). Note them so curriculum data can reference cleanly.

## 1. The Kingdom of God — 하나님 나라

*The uncontested center of Jesus's preaching; threads from the Davidic throne to the throne of the Lamb.*

- **Anchor (memorized):** Mark 1:15 — 마가복음 1:15. Jesus's first public words: the time is fulfilled, the kingdom is at hand.
- **Orbit:**
  - **Origin** — 2 Samuel 7:16 — 사무엘하 7:16 · an everlasting throne promised to David.
  - **Unfolding** — Daniel 7:13–14 — 다니엘 7:13–14 · the Son of Man given everlasting dominion.
  - **Moment** — Luke 17:20–21 — 누가복음 17:20–21 · the kingdom is in your midst.
  - **Aftermath** — Colossians 1:13 — 골로새서 1:13 · transferred into the kingdom of the beloved Son.
  - **Consummation** — Revelation 11:15 — 요한계시록 11:15 · the kingdom of the world becomes the Lord's.
- **The thread:** the reign God always promised arrives in the person of the King.

## 2. Love — 사랑

*The heart of the Law in Jesus's own summary; threads from the Shema to the eternal communion of God with his people.*

- **Anchor (memorized):** John 13:34 — 요한복음 13:34. A new commandment: love one another as I have loved you.
- **Orbit:**
  - **Origin** — Deuteronomy 6:5 — 신명기 6:5 · love the LORD with all your heart (the Shema).
  - **Unfolding** — Leviticus 19:18 — 레위기 19:18 · love your neighbor as yourself.
  - **Moment** — Matthew 22:37–40 — 마태복음 22:37–40 · Jesus unites the two as the greatest commandments.
  - **Aftermath** — 1 John 4:10 — 요한일서 4:10 · love is God sending his Son for us.
  - **Consummation** — Revelation 21:3 — 요한계시록 21:3 · God dwells with his people forever.
- **The thread:** the love commanded in the Law is the love embodied at the cross.

## 3. Obedience — keeping his word — 순종 · 말씀을 지킴

*"His teachings on the importance of obedience of his word"; threads from covenant obedience to Spirit-enabled, love-shaped obedience.*

- **Anchor (memorized):** John 14:15 — 요한복음 14:15. If you love me, you will keep my commandments.
  - *Alt anchor (more vivid for memorization):* Matthew 7:24 — the one who hears these words and does them.
- **Orbit:**
  - **Origin** — Exodus 19:5 — 출애굽기 19:5 · if you obey my voice and keep my covenant.
  - **Unfolding** — Ezekiel 36:26–27 — 에스겔 36:26–27 · a new heart and my Spirit to walk in my statutes.
  - **Moment** — Matthew 7:24–25 — 마태복음 7:24–25 · the wise builder hears and does.
  - **Aftermath** — Romans 8:3–4 — 로마서 8:3–4 · the law's requirement fulfilled in those who walk by the Spirit.
  - **Consummation** — Revelation 14:12 — 요한계시록 14:12 · the saints who keep God's commands and hold to Jesus.
- **The thread:** obedience is not cold law-keeping but love made possible by the Spirit Christ gives.

## 4. Prayer — 기도

*Taught and modeled constantly; threads from the first cry to God to the prayers of the saints rising before the throne.*

- **Anchor (memorized):** Matthew 7:7 — 마태복음 7:7. Ask, and it will be given; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened.
- **Orbit:**
  - **Origin** — Genesis 4:26 — 창세기 4:26 · people begin to call on the name of the LORD.
  - **Unfolding** — Jeremiah 29:12–13 — 예레미야 29:12–13 · call on me, seek me with all your heart, and I will hear (mirrors the anchor's seek/find).
  - **Moment** — Matthew 6:9–13 — 마태복음 6:9–13 · Jesus gives the pattern (the Lord's Prayer). *Read by Rhema, not memorized.*
  - **Aftermath** — Hebrews 4:16 — 히브리서 4:16 · draw near to the throne of grace with confidence (Christ our High Priest).
  - **Consummation** — Revelation 8:3–4 — 요한계시록 8:3–4 · the prayers of the saints rise before God.
- **The thread:** prayer is access to the Father, opened wide by Christ the High Priest.

## 5. Faith — 믿음

*The classic Romans/Hebrews thread; from Abraham believing to Christ the founder and perfecter of faith.*

- **Anchor (memorized):** John 6:29 — 요한복음 6:29. This is the work of God: that you believe in him whom he has sent.
  - *Alt anchor (warmer/simpler):* Mark 9:23 — all things are possible for one who believes.
- **Orbit:**
  - **Origin** — Genesis 15:6 — 창세기 15:6 · Abraham believed, and it was counted as righteousness.
  - **Unfolding** — Habakkuk 2:4 — 하박국 2:4 · the righteous shall live by his faith.
  - **Moment** — Mark 9:23 — 마가복음 9:23 · all things are possible for one who believes.
  - **Aftermath** — Romans 5:1 — 로마서 5:1 · justified by faith, we have peace with God.
  - **Consummation** — Hebrews 12:1–2 — 히브리서 12:1–2 · Jesus, the founder and perfecter of our faith.
- **The thread:** the faith Abraham began finds its author and finisher in Jesus.

## Ordering rationale

Sequenced by Jesus's emphasis, filtered for threadability:
**Kingdom** (his unmistakable center) → **Love** (the heart of the Law) →
**Obedience** (love proven) → **Prayer** (the believer's breath) →
**Faith** (the ground of it all). Money/possessions, though high-frequency in
the Gospels, is set aside here: its tie to Christ is more ethical-thematic than
a single clean OT→NT arc, so it fits the personal library better than a curated
thread.
